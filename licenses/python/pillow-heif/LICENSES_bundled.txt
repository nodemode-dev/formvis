formvis does not ship pillow-heif's PyPI binary wheels, which link the GPL x265 encoder. The desktop engine ships
pillow-heif 1.8.1 built from its source archive (SHA-256 pinned) by setup/heif_decode.py, against these two libraries,
built unmodified from their release archives with decoders only: no encoder, no other codec, no plugin loading. The
freeze scripts refuse an engine that holds any x265 or x264 library, and the engine's --selfcheck refuses one whose
libheif has a decoder other than libde265 or an encoder other than mask.

A build made with FORMVIS_HEIF=0 leaves pillow-heif and both libraries out, and opens no HEIC or HEIF.
